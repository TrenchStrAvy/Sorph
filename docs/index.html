Everyone speaks their own language. Everyone is understood.

That is what Sorph is for: to bring people together across every language, privately, on your own machine. No account, no cloud, nothing to pay.

Hold a key, speak, let go. Your words are already in the field you were typing in, in any of 99 languages.

Sorph runs entirely on your Mac, so your voice stays on it: no account, no cloud, nothing to pay. The sphere is Sorph at work, moving with your voice while you hold the key.

Get Sorph

git clone https://github.com/TrenchStrAvy/sorph.git && cd sorph && ./install.sh

macOS 13 or later, Python 3.10 or later and about 1 GB free: the speech models for every language are downloaded once (0.8 GB), then everything runs offline. Translate mode adds 2.5 GB the first time you turn it on. The installer sets up the models, a Sorph runtime app, a Launchpad icon and a login service. Grant Microphone, Accessibility and Input Monitoring once, then hold Right‑Option and speak.

Already have Sorph? One command updates it and keeps your permissions and settings: curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TrenchStrAvy/Sorph/main/update.sh | zsh
